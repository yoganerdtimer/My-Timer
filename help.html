🧘 Yoga Timer - Help & User Guide

App version 2.0

Welcome to Yoga Timer

Yoga Timer helps you stay focused during yoga practice by timing each pose and ringing a bell at transitions. Create custom flows with one or more sections, each with its own poses and intervals.

What’s new in 2.0: Redesigned running timer with wavy progress bars, multi-section sessions, a first-run onboarding guide, improved landscape layout, and bell sound tied to your device alarm volume.

Creating a Timer

Start the wizard

  1. On first launch, a welcome screen appears once with tips and a Create Timer button (or tap Cancel to go to an empty timer list)
  2. After that, tap the green + button next to My Yoga Timers to add more timers
  3. Enter a timer name (e.g. “Morning Flow”)
  4. Choose how many sections your session has (1–7) using the colored number chips
  5. Tap Next to configure each section

Configure each section

For every section you can set:

  • Section name (e.g. “Warm up”, “Standing poses”)
  • Number of poses (1–100) using the slider, stepper, or quick picks: 5, 20, 30, 40, 50
  • Interval per pose (3 seconds to 20 minutes) using the slider, stepper, or quick picks: 10s, 45s, 1m, 1m30s, 2m

Use Next to move through sections. On the last section, tap Finish (or Save when editing).

💡 Tip: A single-section timer works like a classic yoga timer. Use multiple sections when you want different pose counts or intervals for warm-up, main practice, and cool-down.

Starting & Running a Session

Start

  1. Find your timer in My Yoga Timers
  2. Tap the green Play button on the timer card
  3. The active session appears at the top of the list with a bell at the first pose

During a session

Active session display shows:

  • Current pose — time remaining, wavy progress bar, and “Pose X of Y”
  • Total session time remaining — overall countdown and session progress bar
  • Sections list — all sections with the current one highlighted
  • Controls — Pause (amber), Resume (green), and Stop (red)

Landscape mode

When you rotate to landscape during a session:

  • Multi-section timers: Progress bars use most of the screen (about 72% width); a compact sections list on the right shows the section number and name, with the current section highlighted
  • Single-section timers: The sections list is hidden; progress bars use the full width for a cleaner view

Portrait layout is unchanged and always shows the full sections list when your timer has more than one section.

Pause, resume, and stop

  • Pause — temporarily stops the countdown; session state is saved
  • Resume — continues from where you left off
  • Stop — ends the session and clears active timer state
  • When the session completes, tap the red Done button
💡 Tip: You can switch apps or lock the screen while a session runs. The timer keeps going in the background, and the bell still plays at pose changes (if sound is enabled).

Managing Timers

Edit a timer

  1. Tap the timer card, or open the ⋮ menu and choose Edit
  2. Walk through the same wizard to change the name, section count, or section details
  3. Tap Save when finished
⚠️ Note: If you edit a timer that is currently running, the session restarts from the beginning with the new settings.

Delete a timer

  1. Open the ⋮ menu on the timer card
  2. Choose Delete

Settings

Open settings

Tap the gear icon (⚙️) at the top of the main screen. Settings open as an overlay; use the back arrow or your device’s back button to return to your timers.

App settings

  • Sound: Bell at each pose transition (uses your device alarm volume; respects Do Not Disturb / call state where applicable)
  • Dark Mode: Light or dark theme

Ads & in-app purchase (Remove ads)

What kind of ads

Yoga Timer uses Google AdMob App Open ads—full-screen ads that may appear when you open the app (often after a cold start, when the app was not running in the background). Ads load only when your device is online.

How often (48-hour schedule)

  • First ad: No earlier than 48 hours after the app records your first launch on this installation
  • Later ads: At least 48 hours after the previous ad was shown

If an ad fails to load (e.g. no network), nothing is counted as shown—you may see an ad on a later launch when the schedule allows.

Remove ads (one-time purchase)

In Settings, buy Remove ads once through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment; we do not receive your full card number.

  • After purchase, the app stops requesting App Open ads
  • Use Restore purchase if you reinstall or use another device signed in to the same Google account

Install the app from the Google Play Store for purchases to work as intended.

Understanding the Display

Pose progress

  • Countdown for the current pose
  • Wavy gradient progress bar for the current pose interval
  • Percentage complete for the current pose

Session progress

  • Total session time remaining for the entire flow (all sections)
  • Session progress bar and overall completion percentage

Sections list

Each section shows its name, pose count, and interval (portrait). The active section is highlighted with a colored border and background. In landscape, the list is compact—badge and name only—and hidden entirely for single-section timers.

Example: One section with 10 poses at 30s each = 5 minutes total. The bell rings at the start of each new pose.

Tips for Best Experience

  • Prepare your space before you tap Play
  • Keep the device nearby so you can hear the bell without watching the screen
  • Use quick picks when creating timers for common pose counts and intervals
  • Try landscape on a tablet or stand to see progress and sections side by side
  • Start with shorter intervals and increase as you progress

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use the app in the background?

A: Yes. A running session continues when you switch apps or lock the screen. A notification shows session progress while the timer runs.

Q: What if I close the app during a session?

A: Progress is saved automatically. When you reopen the app, the active session resumes if it was still running or paused.

Q: Can I have multiple timers?

A: Yes. Create as many timers as you like for different practices.

Q: Why don't I hear the bell?

A: The bell uses your device alarm volume (not media or ringtone). Turn up alarm volume, ensure Sound is on in Settings, and check that the device is not in silent or Do Not Disturb mode (or in a call, where the app may skip the bell).

Q: Does the app work offline?

A: Yes for timers. Creating and running sessions, sounds, and settings work offline. Internet is needed only for ads (if you have not purchased Remove ads), in-app purchase or restore, or opening Help/Privacy in a browser.

Q: How often do ads appear?

A: About every 48 hours on a rolling schedule, if you have not bought Remove ads, you are online, and an ad loads successfully.

Q: How do I remove ads?

A: Open Settings → Remove ads (one-time purchase). Use Restore purchase after reinstall or on another device with the same Google account.
